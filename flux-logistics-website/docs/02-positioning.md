# Positioning, Value Proposition & Headline Decision

## Core positioning statement

> Flux Logistics is a responsive, owner-led logistics agency backed by Landstar's transportation
> network, helping customers manage specialized, urgent, hands-on and operationally sensitive freight.

## Positioning statement (site-facing)

> Specialized freight support for shipments that require more than dock-to-dock transportation.

## Supporting statement

> Flux Logistics coordinates nationwide capacity for driver-assisted deliveries, carpet and flooring
> products, hazmat freight, oversized loads, expedited shipments, drop trailer programs and other
> freight requiring hands-on planning and execution.

These two lines are used verbatim in `src/data/site.ts` (`site.tagline`, `site.supportingStatement`) and
are the canonical description used across meta descriptions, the homepage, and the About page.

## Core value proposition

Shippers with freight that doesn't fit a standard dock-to-dock template, driver-assisted unloading,
flooring/carpet-padding products, hazmat, oversized, expedited, or recurring specialized lanes, get:

1. **A direct, owner-level point of contact** (Tyler McNutt) instead of a rotating dispatch queue or
   call center, for the entire life of the shipment.
2. **Terminology and process that actually matches how the freight moves** (driver assist vs. driver
   unload vs. lumper vs. customer unload), reducing the risk of a mispriced or mis-staffed load.
3. **Access to Landstar's large, vetted carrier network**, so the responsiveness of a small agency
   doesn't come at the cost of capacity or specialized equipment access.
4. **Requirements confirmed before the truck moves**, not discovered by the driver on arrival, which
   reduces detention, disputes, and failed deliveries.

## Headline decision

The brief supplied five candidate headlines for evaluation:

| # | Headline | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Freight Solutions for Difficult, Urgent and Hands-On Shipments | Accurate but generic ("Freight Solutions" is category boilerplate); leads with the customer's problem framing more than a differentiated identity. |
| 2 | When Your Freight Requires More Than a Truck | Strong hook, but as a standalone H1 it's vague about *what* Flux Logistics actually does until the subhead — riskier for a visitor who scans and leaves. |
| 3 | Specialized Freight. Hands-On Service. Nationwide Capacity. | Three concrete claims in fragment form: what (specialized freight), how (hands-on service), and the reassurance broker-shoppers look for (nationwide capacity, i.e., "you won't lose access to trucks by going small"). Scannable, keyword-relevant, and directly answers the "are you too small for my freight?" objection. |
| 4 | Transportation Built Around the Way Your Freight Actually Moves | Good supporting-line material; too abstract to lead with — doesn't say what kind of freight or what Flux Logistics does. |
| 5 | From Driver-Assisted Deliveries to Specialized Freight | Leads with the differentiator (good instinct) but reads narrower than the full service range, and "From X to Y" undersells everything that isn't at either end of that range. |

**Decision: Option 3 — "Specialized Freight. Hands-On Service. Nationwide Capacity."**

Rationale: it is the only option that (a) states what kind of freight Flux Logistics handles, (b) states
*how* it's handled (hands-on, not self-serve), and (c) preempts the single biggest objection an
owner-led agency faces, "can you actually cover my freight nationwide, or am I trading capacity for
service?", in the same breath. It's also naturally keyword-relevant ("specialized freight") without
reading as SEO-stuffed. Options 2 and 5 are held in reserve as strong subheads/section headlines
elsewhere on the site (Option 2's spirit is used as the Driver-Assisted Freight section framing on the
homepage).

**Homepage subheadline** (used as-is, per the brief's supporting statement):

> Flux Logistics coordinates nationwide capacity for driver-assisted deliveries, carpet and flooring
> products, hazmat freight, oversized loads, expedited shipments, drop trailer programs and other
> freight requiring hands-on planning and execution.

## What Flux Logistics is *not* positioned as

- Not a generic "we specialize in everything" brokerage — the site never claims blanket expertise
  across all freight types.
- Not a guarantee that any given driver will unload any given load — every driver-assist mention on the
  site is paired with "subject to carrier and driver approval," consistent with the compliance
  requirements.
- Not the owner of Landstar equipment or a Landstar-badged entity — always "an independent agency of
  Landstar."
